Date Change Policy
  • Date changes are subject to the airline's fare rules, availability on the new date, and any applicable change fee and fare difference, which will be disclosed and authorized before the change is processed.

  • Name changes are not permitted as part of a date change request.
  • Once processed, a revised e-ticket and confirmation will be issued reflecting the new flight date and time.
